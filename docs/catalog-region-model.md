# ELIFORA catalog region model

Regions are exact documentary scopes: an ISO two-letter market, explicit `EU` or explicit `GLOBAL`, or `UNKNOWN`. A language or an unqualified `.com` does not imply a global SKU or global technical permission.

The initial intake has US, CA, GB, ES and UNKNOWN scopes. Explicit URL market selectors justify US/CA/GB/ES source scope; generic Matrix, Redken, Keune and Revlon origins, and the unqualified Wella technical PDF path remain UNKNOWN. This is documentary scope, not a claim that every product is currently sold throughout a jurisdiction.

One stable line can have several separately sourced regional entry revisions. Thus 29 lines produce 30 regional entries. Wella Color Touch has separate US page and UNKNOWN technical-PDF records. Goldwell evidence is CA only; an obsolete PDF that redirected to a country selector was excluded. Counts must not sum these into distinct lines.

Composite foreign keys require source and entry regions to match; a trigger also requires the source's brand to match the line. Exact region filters never inherit US, EU, UNKNOWN or GLOBAL records for Türkiye. UNKNOWN cannot advertise executable support. A proposed technical binding must match every product's explicit country_region, brand identity and line identity. Even GLOBAL is not automatically a substitute for any requested country; an explicit applicability decision is required in a future reviewed technical pack.

Hair regions (root/length/end) are unrelated to manufacturer market scope. Existing signed plans and governed recipes retain their existing hair-region and manufacturer restrictions. No geographic inference or alteration of those contracts was introduced.
