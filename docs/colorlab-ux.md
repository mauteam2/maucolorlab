# ELIFORA ColorLab UX

ColorLab is a technical workbench: customer and hair → target and evidence → technical evaluation → controlled recipe and application. The workflow rail indicates the current presentation stage; it is not an execution gate.

## Target

The existing canonical target model remains intact. The quick builder orders depth before tone family and region. Secondary direction, warmth, grey coverage, preservation and correction remain in their regional editor. Presets write the same structured target as the detailed controls. Swatches are text-labeled illustrations of a color direction, never hair simulation, a product match or a promise of an achievable result.

## Evidence and evaluation

The pre-evaluation summary exposes recorded and missing fields per region. Risk and evidence confidence are visually separate. Feasibility receives a state-appropriate notice. “Neden bu yol?” displays deterministic engine reasons, followed by ordered technical strategy, checkpoints, physical tests, missing evidence and recipe availability. Low risk is not equivalent to complete evidence. A blocked result still explains what must happen next.

Unknown, stale and conflicting inputs are not repaired in the UI. Engine-provided requests to refresh evidence or review conflicts remain visible. Multi-session plans retain the desired target and the explicit inter-session reassessment boundary.

## Catalog and controlled recipe

The information-only brand discovery selector is secondary to the technical workflow and never writes a catalog or product ID into the recipe. The unavailable state reads:

> Bu seri ELIFORA kataloğunda yer alıyor ancak doğrulanmış teknik reçete desteği henüz yayınlanmadı.

The full brand library is region-specific. Source-collected, reviewed and published states are distinct; a source receipt alone is not technical authorization.

The stored controlled recipe is a formulation document: approved series, shade/developer, professional-selected paint grams, verified ratio, calculated developer grams, total mixture, source links and immutable version. The UI renders stored arithmetic; it does not calculate authority, infer unverified pigment coefficients, choose grams for the professional or bypass required review. Live remains a separate explicit professional approval.
