# ELIFORA Salon Intelligence V1

Intelligence is deterministic, source-linked observation. It never predicts churn,
revenue, staffing, pricing, demand, health or product recommendations. There is no
employee score or autonomous copilot, messaging, booking or permission change.

Signals include current low-stock count, canonical CRM return-window clients with
no future appointment, incomplete direct-cost count, configured known-revenue-cost
coverage threshold and configured cancellation-rate threshold. Each has its metric,
value, exact period/snapshot context, denominator/formula, source domain and bounded
detail action. Thresholds are explicit nullable management configuration; no
universal business threshold is imposed. Version conflict rejects a stale change.

Actions navigate to existing Stock, CRM, appointment, commission, technical session
or cost provenance workflows. No insight executes a transaction. A PENDING or
STALE_CALENDAR read model is labelled with its update time and pending events;
observations from it are not described as guaranteed real-time truth.

The approved cream/plum/copper design, integrated f/brush logo and sign-in intro
remain intact. Reports use stacked cards and source rows at 320px, exact monetary
text, visible unavailable states and explicit compared dates. Large decorative
charts and dense mobile tables are avoided; every numeric value is textual and
available to assistive technology.
